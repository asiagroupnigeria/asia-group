Company Overview

Built on decades of manufacturing excellence.

Asia Group of Companies is the premier distribution partner and largest off-taker of Aspira products across the African continent. Aspira Nigeria Limited the manufacturing arm has operated in Kano since 2009 as a key subsidiary of the Lee Group of Companies, a Hong Kong-based multinational with over 60 factories in Nigeria alone.

Founded by Alhaji Sani Isah, Asia Group's footprint spans FMCG, pharmaceuticals, automotive and logistics creating an unmatched closed-loop supply chain from Aspira's Kano factory to Lekki Deep Sea Port and on to global markets.
