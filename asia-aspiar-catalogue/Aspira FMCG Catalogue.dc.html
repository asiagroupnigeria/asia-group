Company Overview

Built to supply, not just to sell.

Aspira FMCG is the home-and-personal-care division of ASIA Group of Co. Ltd, manufacturing detergents, soaps and petroleum-jelly derived products for retail, institutional and private-label buyers. Since 2011 we have shipped finished goods and bulk formulations to over 40 countries, with dedicated export desks serving North American and UK accounts.

Three vertically integrated facilities give us control over raw-material sourcing, formulation and fill lines — so lead times and unit economics stay predictable even as order volumes scale.
