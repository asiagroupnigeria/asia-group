In a gesture that speaks to the culture of respect and mentorship at the heart of Nigerian enterprise, Asia Group's CEO paid a warm courtesy visit to Alhaji Dahiru Barau Mangal CON — one of Nigeria's most distinguished industrialists, philanthropists, and business icons.

Alhaji Mangal, the founder of Max Air and a prominent figure across Nigeria's aviation, oil and gas, real estate, and manufacturing sectors, is a towering example of what determination and integrity can build. Conferred with the national honour of Commander of the Order of the Niger (CON), his journey from modest beginnings to the heights of Nigerian industry is an inspiration to entrepreneurs across the continent.

### A Visit Rooted in Respect

The visit was a social felicitation call — a tradition deeply valued in Northern Nigerian culture, where senior business leaders are honoured not just for their commercial achievements, but for their wisdom, their example, and their contributions to society.

For Asia Group's CEO, the visit was an opportunity to personally convey respect and warm regards to a man whose life and work have helped shape the landscape of Nigerian enterprise as it is known today.

> "There is much we can learn from those who came before us and built great things against all odds. Visiting Alhaji Mangal is a reminder of the values — hard work, faith, community — that must underpin everything we build at Asia Group."

### The Bonds of Nigerian Business Culture

Within Northern Nigeria's business community, the maintenance of personal relationships between business leaders is not merely ceremonial — it is the foundation upon which trust, collaboration, and mutual support are built. Asia Group's leadership takes these bonds seriously, regularly engaging with peers, mentors, and community leaders as part of how the Group conducts itself.

Alhaji Mangal's warmth and hospitality were characteristically generous, and the visit was an enriching exchange for all present.

### Building on Shoulders of Giants

Asia Group's own journey — from a trading enterprise in Kano to a diversified conglomerate spanning FMCG, pharmaceuticals, logistics, and agribusiness — is part of the same Northern Nigerian entrepreneurial tradition that figures like Alhaji Mangal have helped define.

The Group draws strength from the legacies of those who built before it, and remains committed to carrying that tradition forward — with integrity, ambition, and a deep sense of purpose.
