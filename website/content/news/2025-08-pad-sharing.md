In a powerful demonstration of Asia Group's commitment to community wellbeing and girls' education, the Group's CEO personally led a comprehensive sanitary pad distribution initiative across multiple schools in Kano State — reaching thousands of female students and championing a cause that is too often left unaddressed.

The initiative saw Asia Group distribute feminine hygiene products directly to female students in secondary schools across Kano, with the CEO on the ground at distribution points to ensure the programme was carried out with the care and dignity it deserves.

### Why This Initiative Matters

In many communities across Northern Nigeria, access to sanitary products remains a significant barrier to girls' consistent school attendance. When menstrual hygiene products are unavailable or unaffordable, young women are often forced to miss school during their cycle — disrupting their education, limiting their potential, and deepening cycles of disadvantage.

Asia Group's pad-sharing initiative directly addresses this barrier, providing a practical, immediate solution while also sending a powerful message: every girl's education matters, and no student should have her future limited by a lack of access to basic hygiene.

> "A young girl sitting in a classroom is an investment in the future of our communities, our state, and our nation. We cannot speak of development and leave half of our population behind. This is not charity — this is a responsibility."
>
> — Asia Group CEO

### Thousands of Students Reached

The initiative spans multiple schools across Kano State, with this visit being one of many stops in the programme. Thousands of female students have been reached across the distribution drive, each receiving sanitary pads accompanied by the message that they are seen, valued, and supported.

At each school, the distribution was conducted in coordination with school principals, teachers, and parent representatives — ensuring the programme was delivered in a manner that was respectful, inclusive, and sensitive to the community's values.

### Beyond Distribution — Building Awareness

Alongside the physical distribution, the initiative also aims to normalise conversations around menstrual health in school settings. Teachers and school administrators were engaged as partners in this effort — equipped to continue the conversation and support their students throughout the year.

Asia Group believes that lasting change requires more than a one-time donation; it requires shifting culture, building awareness, and empowering schools to become safe spaces where every student — regardless of gender — can learn without barriers.

### Part of Asia Group's Broader CSR Vision

This pad-sharing initiative sits within Asia Group's wider Corporate Social Responsibility framework, which prioritises education, healthcare, and community empowerment across Northern Nigeria. The Group is committed to ensuring that its commercial success translates directly into positive, tangible impact in the communities where it operates.

The initiative will continue to expand across more schools and communities, reflecting Asia Group's belief that business and social responsibility are not in tension — they are inseparable.
