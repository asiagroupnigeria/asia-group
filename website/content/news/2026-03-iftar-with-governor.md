During the blessed month of Ramadan 2026, Asia Group's CEO joined a distinguished gathering of Kano State's business community as a guest of His Excellency, Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf, at the annual Government House Iftar dinner.

The evening brought together leading entrepreneurs, industrialists, and public figures for a night of fellowship, breaking of the fast, and meaningful dialogue about the shared responsibility of building a stronger Northern Nigeria.

### An Evening of Fellowship and Purpose

The Iftar gathering, held in the open air of the Government House grounds, was a powerful reminder of the bond between public leadership and private enterprise. As the Adhan rang out and the fast was broken, conversations naturally turned to community, gratitude, and the work still to be done for the people of Kano State.

The CEO took the opportunity to address fellow business leaders and dignitaries, speaking on the role that responsible business plays in uplifting communities — a theme deeply aligned with Asia Group's founding ethos.

> "Ramadan calls us to remember those who have less than we do. At Asia Group, this is not a seasonal reminder — it is a year-round commitment embedded in how we do business and how we serve our communities."

### Public-Private Dialogue

Beyond the spiritual dimension, evenings such as this serve as an important forum for candid dialogue between the government and the private sector. The Governor used the gathering to reaffirm his administration's commitment to a business-friendly Kano State, while business leaders shared their perspectives on the infrastructure, policy, and investment climate needed to drive growth.

Asia Group's CEO engaged productively with fellow entrepreneurs and government officials, continuing the relationship of open dialogue that has come to define the Group's approach to stakeholder engagement.

### Community at the Core

Asia Group's presence at the Governor's Iftar dinner reflects the Group's deep roots in Kano and its broader commitment to the community fabric of Northern Nigeria. From charitable distributions during Ramadan to year-round CSR initiatives, Asia Group sees its role as extending far beyond commerce — into the very heart of the communities it serves.

The Group continues to honor the spirit of this sacred month through acts of generosity, community outreach, and by living the values upon which it was built.
