In a momentous development for industrial infrastructure and mobility in Northern Nigeria, Asia Group has officially commissioned its premier automotive showroom and integrated after-sales service complex in Kano, established in partnership with Mikano Motors (the automotive division of Mikano International).

The landmark facility marks the full realization of the strategic distribution and service agreement between the two conglomerates, solidifying **Asia Automobiles** as the authorized distributor and service partner for Mikano’s acclaimed global vehicle lineup across Kano and the northern states.

### High-Level Commissioning & Official Signing

The commissioning ceremony was headlined by the Chairman and CEO of Asia Group, **Alhaji Sani Isa Abubakar**, and the Managing Director of Mikano Motors, **Mr. Ralph Haidar**, who finalized the operational protocols and formalized the showroom's official opening.

The signing took place before an audience of senior executives, industry stakeholders, and commercial partners, underscoring both organisations' steadfast dedication to expanding automotive excellence and long-term capital investment in Kano State.

> "When we announced our partnership with Mikano Motors, we committed to building a facility that matches the highest global automotive standards right here in Kano. Today, that commitment is a living reality. This showroom and service hub will provide individuals, corporate fleets, and government institutions with seamless access to world-class vehicles and dependable maintenance without needing to travel outside our region."
>
> — **Alhaji Sani Isa Abubakar**, Chairman & CEO, Asia Group

### World-Class Architectural Grandeur

The new Kano showroom sets a new architectural and experiential benchmark in Northern Nigeria’s automotive landscape:
* **Grand Exhibition Gallery:** A sprawling vehicle showcase floor featuring high-gloss marble reflective flooring, ambient architectural illumination, and dedicated staging for luxury SUVs, rugged pickups, and executive sedans.
* **Classical Decorative Craftsmanship:** Masterfully detailed columns adorned with gold-leaf accents and bespoke moulding, reflecting the dignity and heritage of Kano's commercial pedigree.
* **VIP Consultation & Executive Lounges:** Soundproofed, glass-enclosed client negotiation suites designed for private corporate and institutional consultations.
* **Leadership Wall of Honour:** Prominently mounted at the main entrance, portraits honouring President Bola Ahmed Tinubu GCFR, Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf, Emir of Kano Alhaji Aminu Ado Bayero, and Chairman Alhaji Sani Isa Abubakar — alongside commemorative portraits celebrating the joint leadership of Mikano and Asia Group.

### Delivering Global Brands to Northern Nigeria

Through this flagship facility, Asia Automobiles delivers Mikano Motors' premier lineup directly to northern consumers and corporate clients:
* **Changan Auto:** Featuring the acclaimed UNI series (UNI-K, UNI-T), the robust CS95 7-seater luxury SUV, CS55 Plus, and the versatile Hunter pickup truck.
* **Maxus Commercial & Passenger:** Including the rugged Maxus T90 4x4 pickup, the D90 executive full-size SUV, and specialized passenger vans.
* **GWM Tank:** The prestigious Tank 500 luxury off-roader, combining formidable all-terrain performance with top-tier interior luxury.
* **Heavy Haulage & Industrial Solutions:** Serving construction and logistics contractors with SinoTruck heavy haulage prime movers, tippers, and Mikano heavy-duty power generation solutions.

### Integrated After-Sales & Technical Excellence

Beyond vehicle sales, the complex houses a modern after-sales service center equipped with computer-assisted diagnostic bays, dedicated hydraulic vehicle lifts, and fully stocked genuine OEM parts inventories. 

To guarantee customer satisfaction, master automotive technicians trained under Mikano’s factory-certified standards will oversee all servicing, warranty fulfillment, and scheduled maintenance. The facility also creates high-skilled automotive engineering jobs and apprenticeships, transferring modern technical expertise directly to local Kano youth.

The commissioning of this landmark complex reflects Asia Group's enduring pledge to foster local prosperity, modern infrastructure, and world-class commercial standards in Kano and throughout Nigeria.
